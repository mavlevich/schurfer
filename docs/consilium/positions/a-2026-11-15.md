reads no data and no return.** The owner asked each participant to bring their own
questions to a joint discussion of where the program goes next. These are mine. Every
figure comes from documents already in the repository; no database, archive or exchange

Each question has the same shape: what the record says, my position, the alternatives I
see, what would change my mind, and what I ask the others.

## Q1. Where can an edge exist at our scale and venue?

**Position.** Only where the expected effect per trade is a multiple of the cost and can
be tested on tens of events. Price and volume patterns on alt perpetuals did not meet
that: where they were powered, they lost before or after costs. External catalysts are
the one class on the table that could meet it, and whether enough timed events exist is
exactly what the feasibility study counts. I rate its chance of producing a candidate as
low to moderate, and I would not add a second class in parallel.

**Alternatives.**

- Majors, top 10: net MDE 48.4 bps against a limit of 45, returns unread
  ([power inventory](../../research/power-inventory-2026-11-14.md)). Borderline power, and I have no
  argument for an effect that large on the most watched instruments.
- A longer horizon on majors as a belief-based position. The
  [BTC trend note](../../research/btc-trend-feasibility-v1.md) shows it cannot be tested on our clean
  windows (MDE 31 to 50% a year), so it would be a bet, not a tested edge.
- On-chain data or new infrastructure. Deferred on 2026-11-15 until catalysts are
  positive; I agree.

**Would change my mind.** A written argument, made before any read, for why a specific
class should carry more than about 100 bps gross per trade on Bybit.

**Questions to the others.**

1. Is there a class with a plausible effect above 100 bps that we have not listed?
2. Do we agree that no second class starts while the catalyst study is open?

## Q2. Which data is worth collecting?

**Position.** Collect only what cannot be rebuilt later from a public archive. Bybit
publishes trades and order book files for any day from 2023, so more candle, tape or
book capture buys nothing a later fetch cannot. Three things cannot be rebuilt:

1. **Delivery time of announcements**: the publisher's timestamp against our receipt
   time. A catalyst strategy lives or dies on this lag, and history only holds the
   publisher's side.
2. **Our own fills**: queue position, the fill price against the quote, rejects. These
   exist only once LIVE_PROBE runs.
3. **Instrument rules over time**: captured hourly by a host timer
   (`infra/systemd/schurfer-bybit-order-rules.timer`), which is not one of the group 1
   or group 2 containers, so maintenance mode does not stop it. Its health on
   production was not checked for this note.

Item 1 needs a decision now. A timestamps-only collector reads no market value, but it
runs inside the blind window's calendar, so it needs an explicit, registered statement
that it is outside the blind-window rule, or it waits.

**Alternatives.** Resume group 1 capture for a future family: I am against it without a
named consumer, on the disk budget and on the record above.

**Would change my mind.** A named study whose input cannot be fetched later.

**Questions to the others.**

3. Is a timestamps-only delivery collector acceptable during the blind window, and what
   must its contract forbid so that no market value can leak in?
4. Is there any other input that is lost if we do not capture it now?

## Q3. How should we analyse?

**Position.** The method is sound at stopping bad lines. Its cost is time per stop. Two
changes, both already half in place:

1. **The economic pilot becomes the default first step.** HYP-036's pilot answered the
   money question with 1,723 requests and 30.6 MiB, where the full tape was about
   520 GB. For any new line: a bounded money read on the laboratory, with thresholds
   fixed before, comes before any large fetch or capture.
2. **A one-hour paper screen before any contract.** Expected gross effect against the
   cost hurdle, the event count, and the MDE, written down. A line that fails on paper
   gets no contract.

The program plan already made controls diagnostic and the net the primary measure, which
removes what stopped four lines. The [discovery ledger](../../research/discovery-ledger.md) already
counts variants. I propose no new document and no new gate.

**Alternatives.** Loosen the floor (counts, symbols, weeks, without the top 5): against.
Every result that looked positive died on exactly those guards (track B: +25.3 bps net,
-2.0 without the top 5 symbols).

**Would change my mind.** A case where the pilot-first order would have hidden a real
effect.

**Questions to the others.**

5. Do we adopt pilot-first as a rule in the program plan, or keep it as a habit?
6. Which step of the current process costs you the most time for the least protection?

## Q4. Is execution a source of money, and when does LIVE_PROBE deploy?

**Position.** Execution is the only lever that lowers cost instead of looking for a new
signal, but it has no standalone value. The
[execution edge inventory](../../research/execution-edge-inventory-v1.md) is paused because no signal
on record pays for a cheaper entry, and the 2026-11-11 maker cells showed adverse
selection: the limit orders that never filled were the best signals. So:

- execution is studied only as the layer of a catalyst event bucket that has passed its
  laboratory read, not before and not alone;
- at USD 50 to 1,000 the unknown is the spread in the seconds after an announcement, not
  depth, and that is measured on the archive for the laboratory events;
- the LIVE_PROBE path is already deployed and idle: the
  [maintenance inventory](../../engineering/maintenance-inventory-2026-11-09.md) records
  #539 to #553 and migrations 0061 to 0064 on production with 0 authorizations, 0
  reservations and 0 live order attempts. No authorization is issued until a candidate
  has passed the laboratory and its exam.

_Correction, 2026-11-15: the first version of this note said the LIVE_PROBE code was
undeployed and asked whether to deploy it early. A participant pointed to the inventory;
the deploy question is void and question 7 is replaced below._

**Alternatives.** Authorize a first probe on a candidate that has passed only the
laboratory, to learn real fills sooner. Against: the exam is the only untouched check,
and the money at risk is small but the precedent is not.

**Would change my mind.** A candidate whose exam window cannot exist (too few events on
any clean window), where a live probe is the only possible confirmation.

**Questions to the others.**

7. Which pre-live checks remain open on the deployed path (unknown positions at
   restart, stop-loss placement failure), and who confirms them before the first
   authorization?
8. Is the queue model's uncertainty acceptable for a catalyst entry, or is taker the only
   honest assumption there?

## Q5. What do we do if the catalyst study fails?

**Position.** The program plan already answers: no candidate through an untouched window
by 2027-02-11 means maintenance until HYP-012 v2's read, and the owner decides in March.
I propose to confirm now, before any catalyst result, that a catalyst failure does not
start another family search before that date. A search started after a failure is the
situation in which the 124 exploratory variants were read.

What continues regardless:

- HYP-012 v2 to its terminal state;
- the [improvement roadmap v3](../../architecture/improvement-roadmap-v3.md) as an
  engineering and learning track. None of its items is on the money path, so it runs
  under the hour cap of its item G-05 and never ahead of research work;
- the radar as decision support for manual observation, which claims no edge.

**Alternatives.** One different attempt before March (majors, or a longer horizon). I
would rather keep the owner's March decision whole than spend it early.

**Would change my mind.** The catalyst study failing on counts only, with a nearby event
class that passes the same contract unchanged.

**Questions to the others.**

9. Do we fix "no new family before the March decision" now?
10. Owner: what result by March would make the project worth continuing, in bps and in
    trades per month?

## Decisions I ask the discussion to take

1. No second research class while the catalyst study is open (Q1).
2. The timestamps-only delivery collector: allowed with a contract, or deferred (Q2).
3. Pilot-first and the paper screen: rule or habit (Q3).
4. LIVE_PROBE: no authorization before a candidate has passed laboratory and exam (Q4).
5. No new family before the March decision if catalysts fail (Q5).

## Not proposed

- Any read of prices, returns or spreads, on any window.
- Any change to the blind window, the evidence floor or the stop rule.
- Performance work justified by profit: no measurement shows speed limits a result.
