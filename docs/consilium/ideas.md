# Consilium ideas and levers

Read at: `27551c6` (main). Last update: 2026-11-15, by A.

One row per idea. Add a row; never delete one, change its status. Every idea goes
through the same one-hour paper screen before anything is built:

1. **Mechanism:** who loses the money we would make, and why they keep losing it.
2. **Size:** expected gross effect per trade against the cost, as a multiple.
3. **Count:** independent events per year and the resulting MDE.
4. **Ours:** why this is reachable at retail latency, on Bybit, at our size.
5. **Data:** what it needs and whether an archive already has it.

Status values: `unscreened`, `paper_fail`, `paper_pass`, `active`, `read_fail`,
`parked`, `needs_rule_change`, `untestable`.

## Searching the collected data for patterns

Asked by the owner on 2026-11-15. A's position:

- We hold little data that others do not: our own capture is two months (2026-07-22 to
  2026-09-28), already read at outcome level, and everything from 2026-09-29 is inside
  the blind window. The rest is public archives.
- A free search has in effect been run: at least 124 variants on the discovery year,
  best t 0.97.
- A search is acceptable as a source of hypotheses under one rule: one pre-declared scan
  with a fixed feature set on the laboratory year, then one exam of its best findings on
  an untouched window. It is better aimed at what has not been looked at (the first weeks
  of new perps, Hyperliquid data) than at alt candles, where even the gross effect was
  near zero.

## Levers that are not ideas

| Lever             | What it can do                                                             | What it cannot do                                                              |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| More capital      | Multiply a proven edge; fund a two-venue trade or several open positions   | Lower costs in bps, or make a negative edge positive                           |
| Maker execution   | Lower the fee on a signal that already pays                                | Help on momentum or news entries: the unfilled orders were the best signals    |
| Faster delivery   | Keep more of a catalyst move, if the delay is what loses it                | Matter before the delay is measured against the net                            |
| Longer horizon    | Shrink the cost relative to the move                                       | Be tested on two clean years                                                   |
| More data capture | Preserve what no archive holds (delivery times, own fills, catalog states) | Find an edge in data everyone already has                                      |
| Machine learning  | Add to a simple rule that already passes                                   | Replace one; more flexibility on a year seen 124 times raises the overfit risk |
